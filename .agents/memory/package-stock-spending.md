---
name: Package stock and spending
description: User requirements for purchase lots, inventory drawdown, and goal-based money motivation.
---

Purchases represent a named alcoholic product bought as a count of containers with a volume per container and a total amount paid. Logging a matching drink should draw its volume from that stock and use the purchase cost to show actual drink spending and estimated savings against reduction goals.

**Why:** The user described package-based tracking with a 24-pack of 12 fl oz Coors Light at 4.2% ABV as the example, and said spending and projected savings should motivate drinking less.

**How to apply:** Keep stock package-based, link drink logs to matching product and ABV, and present goal-based savings as estimates rather than guaranteed outcomes.
